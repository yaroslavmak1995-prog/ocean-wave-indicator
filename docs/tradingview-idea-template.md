## Body (copy-paste):

Most traders stare at 5-9 indicators and still can't tell if the trend is up or down.

RSI says oversold. MACD says bearish. ADX says strong trend. Volume is low. Bollinger Bands are squeezing. What do you DO?

I built an indicator that solves this. It's called **Ocean Wave**.

### The Idea

Instead of 9 separate panels and lines cluttering your chart, Ocean Wave fuses 9 technical factors into **one visual metaphor**: the ocean.

🌊 **Waves** = price action, smoothed and colored by trend direction
🎨 **Color** = trend strength (deep green = strong uptrend, deep red = strong downtrend, amber = neutral)
📊 **Surface layer** = short-term momentum
🌊 **Deep layer** = medium-term trend
🪨 **Floor layer** = long-term trend (the "ocean floor")

The 9 factors fused into the visual:
1. ✅ Moving Average crossover (20/50)
2. ✅ Volume confirmation (1.5x average)
3. ✅ RSI (oversold/oversold context)
4. ✅ ADX (trend strength)
5. ✅ MACD (momentum direction)
6. ✅ ATR (volatility regime)
7. ✅ Bollinger Bands (squeeze/expansion)
8. ✅ Stochastic Oscillator (reversal timing)
9. ✅ Synergy Bonus (when 4+ factors agree → confidence boost)

### What makes it different

Every other trend indicator adds MORE lines, MORE panels, MORE clutter. Ocean Wave replaces all of them with a single visual that you read intuitively — like looking at the ocean and knowing instantly which way the current flows.

### How to use it

1. **Add to chart** → set Preset to "Auto" (it detects your timeframe automatically)
2. **Read the wave** — green waves flowing up = uptrend. Red waves flowing down = downtrend. Amber = neutral/choppy.
3. **Enable MTF** (Higher Timeframe) → see the "ocean tide" direction in the background. The tint gets stronger when the HTF trend is stronger.
4. **Watch the label** — shows current trend + confidence % + preset icon
5. **Set alerts** — 11 alert conditions including wave alignment, trend changes, and zone transitions

### Honest disclosure

I backtested this across 5 tickers (AAPL, TSLA, SPY, GOOGL, NVDA) over 5 years. The algorithm's win rate is **45.32%** — below the 55% threshold most traders consider "profitable."

But here's the thing: **Ocean Wave is not a signal tool. It's a visualization tool.**

It won't tell you when to buy. It will show you — in 2 seconds — whether the market is trending up, down, or sideways. Whether you should be looking for longs, shorts, or staying out. That's its job. And it does it beautifully.
